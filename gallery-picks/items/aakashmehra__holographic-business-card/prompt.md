# Build: Holographic Business Card

An interactive business card that tilts toward your pointer, lifts on hover and flips on click, with nine holographic foils (Shards, Rainbow, Stars, Cosmos, Hearts, Shapes, Gold, Silver, Aurora) set by the effect and intensity props.

The working code is in gallery-picks/items/aakashmehra__holographic-business-card/component.tsx (demo: demo.tsx). Rebuild it inside the Ezyourlife repo in its own style (docs/design.md): cream page, white cards, thick ink outlines (border-2 border-ink) with hard block shadows (shadow-block-sm / shadow-block), Unbounded headings (font-display), Hind body text, solid brand colours (bg-pink, bg-sun, bg-teal, bg-sky, bg-lime), plain words. Use our shadcn parts from src/components/ui where they fit. Keep the behaviour, keyboard and reduced-motion handling.
