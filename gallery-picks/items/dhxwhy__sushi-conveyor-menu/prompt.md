# Build: Sushi Conveyor Belt Menu – Restaurant Hero Section

Sushi Conveyor Belt Menu – Restaurant Hero Section for React and Tailwind CSS (shadcn/ui): a kaiten sushi belt that keeps turning plates round a stadium-shaped loop. It is built to make a restaurant menu something visitors play with: hover to stop the belt, flick to spin it, pick a plate to order. Use it for restaurant and food ordering pages. A physics belt eases to a stop under the pointer and lifts the plate with a name and price chip; a drag scrubs it and a flick spins on with inertia before settling to cruise speed. A picked plate flies into a dialog with details and an Add to order button. Price tiers become colour-coded plate rings with a legend; active, paused, onActiveChange, onOpenChange, onAdd, speed and labels control it. One roving tab stop with arrow keys, a focus-trapped dialog closed by Escape, a pause button, reduced motion that steps plate by plate, light and dark themes. The included demo runs an eight-plate lunch belt with an order counter and makes no requests beyond image loads. Requires React only, with no animation library; the demo adds a lucide-react icon.
