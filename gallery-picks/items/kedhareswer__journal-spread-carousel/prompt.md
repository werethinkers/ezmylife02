# Build: 3D Journal Page Carousel

3D journal page carousel: an open scrapbook spread in the middle with earlier and later pages standing edge-on in two receding stacks. Drag, flick, click a page or use the arrow keys to turn; each turn swings the next spread in and folds the last one shut. Polaroids, taped prints and handwritten notes sit on each page, and it auto-advances until touched.

The working code is in gallery-picks/items/kedhareswer__journal-spread-carousel/component.tsx (demo: demo.tsx). Rebuild it inside the Ezyourlife repo in its own style (docs/design.md): cream page, white cards, thick ink outlines (border-2 border-ink) with hard block shadows (shadow-block-sm / shadow-block), Unbounded headings (font-display), Hind body text, solid brand colours (bg-pink, bg-sun, bg-teal, bg-sky, bg-lime), plain words. Use our shadcn parts from src/components/ui where they fit. Keep the behaviour, keyboard and reduced-motion handling.
