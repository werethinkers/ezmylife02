# Build: Halftone Shader Hero

Halftone shader hero: a WebGL hero card whose background is liquid marbling printed through a halftone dot screen that blooms under the pointer, with a word-by-word serif headline and a typing prompt bar. For AI and SaaS landing pages.

The working code is in gallery-picks/items/kedhareswer__halftone-flow-hero/component.tsx (demo: demo.tsx). Rebuild it inside the Ezyourlife repo in its own style (docs/design.md): cream page, white cards, thick ink outlines (border-2 border-ink) with hard block shadows (shadow-block-sm / shadow-block), Unbounded headings (font-display), Hind body text, solid brand colours (bg-pink, bg-sun, bg-teal, bg-sky, bg-lime), plain words. Use our shadcn parts from src/components/ui where they fit. Keep the behaviour, keyboard and reduced-motion handling.
