# Build: Handwriting Signature Footer

Handwriting signature footer: a white stylus writes one word in flowing script stroke by stroke, lifts and rests beside it, then a thank-you line, a short note, two actions and a quiet legal bar settle in. Built-in single-stroke script glyphs, no fonts or images. For signing flows, checkouts and case studies.

The working code is in gallery-picks/items/kedhareswer__signature-pen-footer/component.tsx (demo: demo.tsx). Rebuild it inside the Ezyourlife repo in its own style (docs/design.md): cream page, white cards, thick ink outlines (border-2 border-ink) with hard block shadows (shadow-block-sm / shadow-block), Unbounded headings (font-display), Hind body text, solid brand colours (bg-pink, bg-sun, bg-teal, bg-sky, bg-lime), plain words. Use our shadcn parts from src/components/ui where they fit. Keep the behaviour, keyboard and reduced-motion handling.
