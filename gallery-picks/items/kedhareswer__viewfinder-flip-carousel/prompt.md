# Build: 3D Flip Image Carousel

3D flip image carousel for React: only the photo in the viewfinder frame lies flat while the rest fold away on their edges in perspective, opening out of the stack as you move, with a small frame pulse as each lands. Dark stage, titles, keyboard arrows and swipe, reduced-motion aware. Use for camera rolls, photo galleries and portfolios.

The working code is in gallery-picks/items/kedhareswer__viewfinder-flip-carousel/component.tsx (demo: demo.tsx). Rebuild it inside the Ezyourlife repo in its own style (docs/design.md): cream page, white cards, thick ink outlines (border-2 border-ink) with hard block shadows (shadow-block-sm / shadow-block), Unbounded headings (font-display), Hind body text, solid brand colours (bg-pink, bg-sun, bg-teal, bg-sky, bg-lime), plain words. Use our shadcn parts from src/components/ui where they fit. Keep the behaviour, keyboard and reduced-motion handling.
