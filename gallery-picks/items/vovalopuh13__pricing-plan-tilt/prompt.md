# Build: Pricing Plan Tilt

Three pricing cards rendered as real 3D objects: they tilt with the cursor, carry a glare that follows it, and lift on hover. A running light circles the selected plan, and the monthly/yearly toggle is a springy pill with a bouncing Save badge.

The working code is in gallery-picks/items/vovalopuh13__pricing-plan-tilt/component.tsx (demo: demo.tsx). Rebuild it inside the Ezyourlife repo in its own style (docs/design.md): cream page, white cards, thick ink outlines (border-2 border-ink) with hard block shadows (shadow-block-sm / shadow-block), Unbounded headings (font-display), Hind body text, solid brand colours (bg-pink, bg-sun, bg-teal, bg-sky, bg-lime), plain words. Use our shadcn parts from src/components/ui where they fit. Keep the behaviour, keyboard and reduced-motion handling.
