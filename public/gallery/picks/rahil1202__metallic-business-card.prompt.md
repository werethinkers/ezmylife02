# Build: MetallicBusinessCard

A premium, reflective 3D metallic business card component that dynamically responds to mouse movement with realistic lighting and tilt effects.
It supports multiple metal finishes (gold, silver, bronze, platinum) and intelligently adjusts text color and glow for perfect readability on each surface.

The working code is in gallery-picks/items/rahil1202__metallic-business-card/component.tsx (demo: demo.tsx). Rebuild it inside the Ezyourlife repo in its own style (docs/design.md): cream page, white cards, thick ink outlines (border-2 border-ink) with hard block shadows (shadow-block-sm / shadow-block), Unbounded headings (font-display), Hind body text, solid brand colours (bg-pink, bg-sun, bg-teal, bg-sky, bg-lime), plain words. Use our shadcn parts from src/components/ui where they fit. Keep the behaviour, keyboard and reduced-motion handling.
